Abstract: Eigenvectors of graph-associated matrices like the
Laplacian, adjacency, and variations of them often appear in
graph-based applications. From spectral clustering of data to
spectral-based redistricting algorithms, eigenvectors prove to be
a useful tool.

In this talk, we will discuss a combinatorial perspective on the
relationship between graph perturbation and eigenvectors. For
various graph operations, we will discuss what conditions are
required for two graphs to share eigenvectors. Moreover, the
classification of these types of graphs will be shown. We will
conclude with avenues for future work.
